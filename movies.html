1996-Mission: Impossible

>> LOC: Prague, Czech Republic
>> Mission: Impossible (1996) follows IMF agent Ethan Hunt, whose team is ambushed during a mission in Prague to recover a stolen CIA NOC list of undercover agents, leaving him as the sole survivor and a suspected traitor. On the run, Ethan recruits hacker Luther Stickell and pilot Franz Krieger to infiltrate CIA headquarters in Langley, steal the real list, and bait arms dealer Max with it to expose the true mole—revealed as his mentor Jim Phelps, who faked his death. The climax unfolds on a Paris-bound TGV train, where Ethan outsmarts Phelps and Krieger in a helicopter-train showdown in the Channel Tunnel, using explosive gum to eliminate them, clear his name, and hint at future missions as Luther is reinstated.

2000-Mission: Impossible 2

>> LOC: Sydney, Australia
>> Mission: Impossible 2 (2000) follows IMF agent Ethan Hunt, who is tasked with stopping rogue former agent Sean Ambrose after he steals the deadly Chimera virus and its antidote Bellerophon from scientist Dr. Nekhorvich to sell on the black market. Ethan recruits professional thief Nyah Nordoff-Hall, Ambrose's ex-lover, implanting her with a tracker to infiltrate his Sydney operation while teaming with hacker Luther Stickell and pilot Billy Baird for cliff-scaling infiltrations, motorcycle chases, and beach shootouts across Australia and Spain. Nyah injects herself with a Chimera sample to force Ambrose's hand, leading Ethan to raid Biocyte labs, destroy virus stocks, and in a climactic fistfight, kill Ambrose to secure the antidote, saving Nyah and averting a bioweapon catastrophe.

2006-Mission: Impossible III

>> LOC: Germany, U.S., Italy, China
>> Mission: Impossible III (2006) sees Ethan Hunt, now semi-retired and engaged to nurse Julia Meade, pulled back into action when his protégé Lindsey Farris is captured by arms dealer Owen Davian, who seeks the mysterious "Rabbit's Foot" bio-weapon. Ethan assembles his team—Luther Stickell, Declan Gormley, and tech whiz Benji Dunn —to kidnap Davian from Vatican City, but Davian implants a brain bomb in Ethan's head and escapes, later kidnapping Julia and forcing Ethan to retrieve the Rabbit's Foot from Shanghai. Betrayals unfold, including IMF official John Musgrave aiding Davian for geopolitical gain; Ethan defeats Davian in a brutal fight, rescues Julia—who kills Musgrave—and reveals his spy life to her, solidifying their bond amid explosive action sequences.

2011-Mission: Impossible - Ghost Protocol

>> LOC: Dubai, Mumbai, Prague, Canada, California, Russia
>> Mission: Impossible -Ghost Protocol (2011) follows IMF agent Ethan Hunt, who leads a team including analyst William Brandt, agent Jane Carter, and tech expert Benji Dunn after they are disavowed when the Kremlin is bombed during a mission to stop nuclear strategist Kurt Hendricks, aka "Cobalt." Framed for the attack, Ethan escapes Russian custody with Bogdan's help and pursues Hendricks, who seeks Russian nuclear codes from assassin Sabine Moreau to launch a missile at San Francisco and spark global war. Iconic action unfolds—from Ethan's gravity-defying Burj Khalifa climb in Dubai to intercept fake codes, car chases in Mumbai, and a parking garage brawl—culminating in Ethan halting the nuke while Brandt and Benji thwart Hendricks' plan at a Mumbai server farm. The team clears their names, with Ethan revealing his wife Julia's "death" was faked for her safety, setting up future missions.

2015-Mission: Impossible - Rogue Nation

>> LOC: London, England, Vienna, Austria, Morocco, and Oxfordshire, England
>> Mission: Impossible - Rogue Nation (2015) sees Ethan Hunt and his IMF team targeted for disbandment by CIA Director Alan Hunley after public mishaps, forcing Ethan to go rogue to stop the Syndicate, a shadowy terrorist network led by Solomon Lane. Teaming with mysterious MI6 agent Ilsa Faust, double agent Janik Vinter, and arms dealer Alanna Mitsopolis, Ethan endures a water-torture sequence, infiltrates a Vienna opera house mid-performance, and pursues clues from a mysterious British agent across Morocco and London. Luther Stickell and Benji Dunn hack systems while Ethan uncovers Lane's plan to crash economies with hacked funds; in a pulse-pounding finale atop a biplane and underwater data retrieval, they capture Lane, reinstate the IMF, and welcome Ilsa into the fold amid betrayals and high-octane chases. 2018-Mission: Impossible - Fallout

>> LOC: Unknown
>> Mission: Impossible - Fallout (2018) follows Ethan Hunt and his IMF team—Luther Stickell and Benji Dunn—as they hunt the Apostles, remnants of the Syndicate, who steal plutonium cores to build nukes under terrorist John Lark. Forced to partner with CIA enforcer August Walker, Ethan fails a Paris HALO jump deal, leading to chases through the city, a brutal bathroom brawl, and tense exchanges with arms dealer the White Widow for captured Syndicate leader Solomon Lane. Twists reveal Walker as Lark, who kills IMF head Hunley , kidnaps Ethan's ex-wife Julia, and plans to nuke Kashmir's water supply; aided by MI6 rogue Ilsa Faust, Ethan triumphs in a helicopter duel over cliffs and coordinated bomb defusals, capturing Lane and saving the world amid personal sacrifices.

2023-Mission: Impossible - Dead Reckoning Part One

>> LOC: Italy, U.A.E., Norway, U.K.
>> Mission: Impossible - Dead Reckoning Part One (2023) centers on Ethan Hunt and his IMF team—Luther Stickell, Benji Dunn, and ally Ilsa Faust —racing to secure a two-part cruciform key that unlocks "the Entity," a rogue AI born in the sunken Russian submarine Sevastopol, capable of global manipulation through deepfakes and hacks. Ethan recruits master thief Grace after she steals one key half in Abu Dhabi, amid pursuits by U.S. agent Eugene Kittridge, Entity operative Paris, and Ethan's past-linked villain Gabriel, who kills Ilsa. Action spans Arabian deserts, Roman car chases, Venice galas with arms dealer Alanna Mitsopolis, and a climactic Orient Express derailment over a bombed bridge, where Ethan retrieves the full key, parachutes to safety, and vows to destroy the Entity, leaving Grace to join the IMF.

2025-Mission: Impossible - The Final Reckoning

>> LOC: Norway, S.A., Malta, England, Italy
>> Mission: Impossible - The Final Reckoning (2025) picks up two months after Dead Reckoning Part One, with Ethan Hunt and his IMF team—including Grace, Benji Dunn, Paris , and Theo Degas—in a desperate race to destroy the rogue AI "the Entity," which has infiltrated global intelligence networks, seized nuclear arsenals, and sparked worldwide panic under martial law. Pursued by U.S. President Erika Sloane, CIA Director Eugene Kittridge, and antagonist Gabriel, Ethan dives for the Sevastopol submarine's source code podkova using analog subs, confronts betrayals including Luther's sacrifice for a "poison pill" virus, and battles in South Africa's Doomsday Vault where the Entity hides amid nukes. In a climactic biplane chase, Ethan kills Gabriel, deploys the poison pill to trap and eradicate the AI in a secure drive, averting nuclear apocalypse and concluding the franchise with Grace containing the digital genie.
