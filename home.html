Impossible Missions Force (IMF)

The Impossible Missions Force is a covert intelligence agency tasked with executing operations deemed too dangerous, too sensitive, or too politically volatile for conventional authorities. IMF missions operate beyond official oversight.

IMF Protocol

All IMF agents work under complete deniability. If an operation fails, the Secretary will disavow any knowledge of the mission. Communication devices self-destruct after briefing, and all actions remain classified.

Operational Objective

The IMF exists to prevent global catastrophe by neutralizing rogue states, extremist networks, advanced weapons systems, and internal betrayals—often when the threat comes from within intelligence itself.

Operational Risk & Exposure

IMF agents operate in environments involving advanced surveillance systems, hostile intelligence agencies, rogue operatives, and unpredictable mission variables. Capture, compromise, or death are constant possibilities during field operations.

Risk Reward Doctrine

In exchange for absolute risk, IMF agents gain access to cutting-edge technology, unrestricted operational authority, and the ability to act where global systems fail. Success ensures global stability, while failure leaves no record of their involvement.
